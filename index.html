NIGHTS AT HAUENSTEIN LYRICS VIDEO

2021/10

Our ways are closely connected with a magical place called Hauenstein (or Horni Hrad) in the Ore Mountains. We spent many years there, whether playing, organizing the Spring SunCastle Festival, volunteering or just boozing, and we met a lot of good people. That's why years ago this song was written and now finally this lyrics video putting together our common memories.
